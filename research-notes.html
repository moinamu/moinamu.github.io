Short, open explanations of optimization algorithms and the choices behind numerical experiments. The first note maps two classical methods for monotone inclusions.

01 · Monotone inclusions

One inclusion, two splitting strategies

For 0 ∈ A(x) + B(x), Tseng's forward–backward–forward step corrects at an intermediate point. The Malitsky–Tam forward–reflected–backward step reuses a previous operator value. The note gives their updates, evaluation counts, standard stepsize regimes, and source papers.
